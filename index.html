A 360° nature simulation designed to recreate the feeling of lying in a field on a warm, sunny day.

Through a machine-made, industrial construction of a natural occurrence, Touch Grass explores the relationship between technology and nature. The work questions how closely artificial environments can replicate the natural world, and whether the continuous advancement of technology could ever make such simulations a sufficient substitute for experiencing nature itself.

Jade Brandt about

Jade Brandt is a Stockholm-based multidisciplinary designer working across art direction, spatial design and live culture. With roots in Stockholm's underground music and club scene, her practice is driven by an interest in creating visual and physical worlds. Her approach is hands-on and collaborative, spanning creative direction, visual development and production through to physical realisation.

Alongside her design practice, she is one half of Sonic Erection, an independent platform for Swedish underground culture working across music, film, print and live events. Through collaborations with artists, collectives and cultural institutions, including Cease2Exist, Fotografiska and Nationalmuseum, the platform creates spaces for alternative cultural expressions and brings independent culture into wider contexts.

With five years of freelance experience across visual communication, moving image and creative production, she is currently pursuing a BFA at Beckmans College of Design in Stockholm. Increasingly drawn to spatial design, scenography and the technical possibilities of physical environments, she is interested in developing new ways of translating creative ideas into shared, tangible experiences.
